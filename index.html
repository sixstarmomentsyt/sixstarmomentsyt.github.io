The best GTA 6 moments, every day.

Six Star Moments is a YouTube channel that finds the funniest, wildest and most impressive GTA 6 moments from players and streamers and shares them as daily Shorts, rated by our mascot Six the Gator. Every Sunday, Wasted Weekly counts down the Top 15 moments of the week.

Creators come first

We only feature a clip after its creator says yes. Every featured clip carries the creator's name on screen and a link in the description, and creators with a YouTube channel are invited as collaborators on the video. Our top contributor each month is named Clipper of the Month.

Want your clip featured, or want one removed? Email sixstarmomentsyt@gmail.com. Removal requests are handled within 48 hours.
