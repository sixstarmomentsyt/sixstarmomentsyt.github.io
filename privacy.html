PRIVACY POLICY

Six Star Moments · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Six Star Moments website and the Six Star Moments pipeline, a private tool the channel owner uses to manage the Six Star Moments YouTube channel. The pipeline is not offered to the public. The only person who signs in to it is the channel owner.

Google and YouTube data

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the pipeline can:

  • upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the Six Star Moments channel;
  • read the channel's own video statistics and analytics (views, average view duration, average percentage viewed);
  • read the channel's name and subscriber count.

This data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to see which videos perform well. It is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The sign-in token is kept on that computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Clip creators

To ask permission and give credit, we keep a record of each clip we consider: the public link, the creator's public username, and the creator's reply to our permission request. This record stays on the channel owner's computer and is used only to credit creators and honour their wishes. To have your information removed, or a clip taken down, email sixstarmomentsyt@gmail.com.

Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and it does not collect any personal information. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Contact

Questions about this policy: sixstarmomentsyt@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
